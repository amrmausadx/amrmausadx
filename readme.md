## Amr Mausad Sauber

Professor of Computer Science (Data Science & AI), Faculty of Science, Menoufia University, Egypt.
Founder and Chairman of the [Computality R&D Association](http://computality.org/).

I have been writing code for more than 30 years, from distributed systems and big-data security to deep learning for speech and medical imaging. I build systems that run in production. I am the sole developer of the C# postgraduate Student Information System used across 20 faculties at Menoufia University.

### Research
- **Speech & audio deep learning:** speaker embeddings, denoising, Arabic speech recognition
- **Security of distributed & AI systems:** threat models for malicious and colluding workers, data protection
- **LLM fine-tuning & generation:** LoRA and GAN/REINFORCE pipelines with experiment tracking
- **Currently exploring:** AI security and red-teaming, with a focus on Arabic/dialectal and speech inputs

### Selected publications
- **SPK2VEC**: Speaker embeddings transformer using hybrid supervised + self-supervised contrastive learning. *Applied Soft Computing*, 2026 (first author)
- **VOCAL-Denoiser**: A focal-based U-Net for robust speech denoising. *Computer Speech & Language*, 2026
- **ArabRecognizer**: Modern Standard Arabic speech recognition inspired by DeepSpeech2, utilizing Franco-Arabic. *Int. J. Speech Technology*, 2024
- **A Novel Hadoop Security Model for Addressing Malicious Collusive Workers.** *Computational Intelligence and Neuroscience*, 2021 (first author)
- **Skeletal scintigraphy image enhancement** based on neutrosophic sets and salp swarm algorithm. *Artificial Intelligence in Medicine*, 2020
